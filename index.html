Indoor hall: 24 stands · Creators Courtyard: 14 Scottish businesses
Barras Art & Design, Glasgow · Thu 10 Sep 2026

    Thank you for shopping small and thinking big.

    Indoors you’ll find the tech ecosystem — founders, studios, programmes and the people who back them. Out in the courtyard, fourteen independent Scottish makers and producers. The night ends, but they keep going — follow them, order from them, tell someone else about them.

    • Something to take home: free headshots at SocialPaps (spot 13), caricatures at spot 10, and face painting by the stage.
    • Mentor Zone is on the left as you come in, next to the bar. The mezzanine and mood bar are on the first floor.
    • Several in the courtyard are trading tonight, not just showing. Bring a card — signal in the yard can be patchy.
    • Courtyard tables 2 and 3 are licensed. ID may be requested.
    • Want an introduction to a founder? Speak to any of the team members and volunteers — they’re around all night.

    Part of TecTonic Night Summit, during Glasgow Tech Week.
